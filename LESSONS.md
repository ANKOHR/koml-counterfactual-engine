# Lessons for KOML

The domain needs a separation between observed baselines and hypothetical
alternatives. Exact arithmetic does not establish a causal outcome. A cheaper
alternative can also take longer: the planted fixture shows a hypothetical
25000-pence reduction alongside a hypothetical 1500-minute increase.

Validated locally: deterministic cost-first comparisons reconcile to planted
truth, and editing a fixture changes the output. Not validated: actual savings,
operator adoption, causal accuracy or willingness to pay. No real-use trial has
been conducted as part of this build.

Operating rule: require explicit provenance, integer units and independent
hand-computed expectations before treating an agent-control output as evidence.
Keep real-use verdicts pending until the evidence window has elapsed.
